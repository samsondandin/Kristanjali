## 12 Official Rules & Guidelines
1. **Group Composition**: 8 to 20 participants. Mixed age groups allowed.
2. **Registration**: Every team must register online or inform organisers in advance. Confirm participation between 5:00 PM and 6:00 PM on event day. Outstanding entry fee in cash accepted in this slot. Online-registered teams given preference.
3. **Time Limit**: 9 minutes total (1 min setup/transition + 7 mins performance + 1 min exit).
4. **Song Selection**: Christmas carols in any language allowed. Songs must be appropriate for the occasion.
5. **Backing & Accompaniment**: Live music and live instruments preferred (keyboard, guitar, cajon, etc.). Instrumental backing tracks allowed if submitted/checked earlier. No pre-recorded vocals allowed.
6. **Performance Guidelines**: Sanctuary decorum suitable for a church event. Choreography/movement allowed but should not distract from singing.
7. **Judging Criteria**: Judging based only on singing and music. No marks awarded for choreography or stage presentation.
8. **Reporting & Confirmation Time**: All teams must report between 5:00 PM and 6:00 PM.
9. **Dress Code**: Modest and neat attire expected.
10. **Discipline**: Respect volunteers, judges, and other teams. Misconduct leads to disqualification.
11. **Judges' Decision**: The judges' decision is final.
12. **Late Arrival**: Groups arriving late will perform at the end.
