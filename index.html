    Participant Handbook

    Competition Guidelines & Reporting

    01

    Group Composition

    Each group must have a minimum of 8 participants and a maximum of 20. Mixed age groups are allowed.

    8–20 Singers • Mixed Age Groups
    02

    Registration & Confirmation

    Every team must register online or inform the organisers in advance. Teams must confirm participation between 5:00 PM and 6:00 PM on event day. Outstanding entry fee may be paid in cash. Online teams get preference.

    Closes 14 Dec • 5–6 PM Slot
    03

    Stage Time Limit

    Each group receives 9 minutes for their performance: 1 min setup + 7 mins singing + 1 min exit.

    Strict 9 Min Duration (1+7+1)
    04

    Song Selection

    Christmas carols in any language are allowed. Songs must be reverent and appropriate for the festive Christian occasion.

    Multilingual • Christ-Centered
    05

    Backing & Accompaniment

    Live music and instruments preferred (keyboard, guitar, cajon, etc.). Instrumental backing tracks are allowed if submitted/checked earlier. No pre-recorded vocals allowed.

    Live or Instrumental Backing
    06

    Performance Guidelines

    Groups must maintain sanctuary decorum suitable for a church event. Choreography or movement is permitted but should not distract from singing.

    Sanctuary Decorum & Reverence
    07

    Judging Criteria

    Judging will be based only on singing and music. No marks will be awarded for choreography, stage presentation, or other non-musical elements.

    100% Singing & Musicality
    08

    Reporting & Soundcheck

    All teams must report between 5:00 PM and 6:00 PM to confirm participation and complete any pending entry-fee payment.

    5:00 PM – 6:00 PM Slot
    09

    Dress Code

    Modest, neat, and dignified attire suitable for church grounds and sacred choral performance is expected from all choir members.

    Modest & Dignified
    10

    Discipline & Conduct

    Participants must respect volunteers, judges, and fellow choir teams. Any misconduct or disruptive behavior may lead to immediate disqualification.

    Mutual Respect & Brotherhood
    11

    Judges' Decision

    The adjudication panel's decision is final, binding, and irrevocable for all categories, awards, and prizes.

    Final & Binding
    12

    Late Arrival Policy

    Groups arriving after the designated reporting window will perform at the end of the schedule, subject to committee discretion.

    Late Choirs Perform at End
    I

    On-Site Reporting & Soundcheck (05:00 PM)

    All registered choirs must report on-site at the OLPS Church Hospitality Desk from 05:00 PM onwards for acoustic soundcheck, team roster verification, and stage sequence lot draw before the 06:00 PM invocation.

    II

    Lyric Sheets & Stage Transition

    Submit 3 printed copies of carol lyrics (with English translation if sung in regional languages) upon reporting. Instrument patching must be completed within 2 minutes.

    III

    Choir Attire & Sanctuary Reverence

    Coordinated festive or liturgical choir attire is required. Hosted within the sacred grounds of OLPS Church, Dharwad, dignified sanctuary decorum is expected from all participants.

    IV

    Parish Hospitality & Supper

    Complimentary refreshments and parish fellowship dinner will be served to all registered choristers and accompanists following the prize distribution ceremony.
